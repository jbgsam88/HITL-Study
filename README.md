# HITL-Study
Hello,

I am Junbryan Samson, a doctoral student attending National University, in pursuit of my degree in Technology Management - Data Science. I am conducting a study for my dissertation, Human-in-the-loop (HITL) in Organizational Decision-Making. What that means is that I aim to find out how the use of AI affects decision-making within an organization. This study would seek to find out when and how AI should be used. The parameters focused on include accuracy, error detection, and trust. These will help me measure decision quality for when and where AI helps the most.
